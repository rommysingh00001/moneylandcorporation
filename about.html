About Moneyland Corporation

Less noise. More genuine property.

Our vision is simple: Gurgaon property information should be useful, structured and trustworthy—not a wall of duplicate listings and fake leads.

Our way of working

Moneyland Corporation is building a Gurgaon-first property portal around a one-property, one-public-listing principle. A property should not appear again and again through duplicate posts. The owner provides the property and ownership information; Moneyland Corporation verifies the submission before it becomes a public listing.

That means the portal may intentionally have fewer listings—but the goal is for those listings to be more genuine and more useful. A buyer can study the property information, contact Moneyland Corporation directly and, where enabled, submit an offer through the bid system.

For owners, the objective is to bring serious enquiries instead of repeated low-quality leads. For buyers, the objective is a cleaner property journey with clearer information and a transparent connection to the transaction.
